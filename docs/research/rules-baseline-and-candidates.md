## Candidate comparison and recommendation

The comparison criteria are cross-rule risk, a complete bounded player task, observable arithmetic and aftermath, and implementation/authoring burden. The physical game plus R is the incumbent. These source cases do not demonstrate a software usability improvement.

| Candidate | Source-derived challenge and consequence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Zero-resource Terror/Kidnap** | R 4.1 explicitly permits Kidnap to fund its accompanying Terror. R 4.3.3 requires M26 Guerrillas > Police and an eligible location; 3.3.4 requires an Underground Guerrilla and payment. Proceeds affect further Terror choices; using the activity changes second-faction permission (2.3.4). | Recommend. Breaks a plausible “pay whole action before any effect” model while remaining one Operation. Randomness, pending obligation, counts and deliberate completion all matter. |
| **Cash-funded continuation** | Government pays for Sweep, exposes Guerrillas, then spends Cash for a free Limited Assault; R 2.3.6/3.1.2. No accompanying Special Activity; paid-operation prerequisite; one Cash benefit per card; Cash ownership and alternative LimOps remain choices. | Strong next pressure but wider first task: complete continuation needs all legally available operation types, movement and Cash transfer/removal, not just a scripted Sweep–Assault. |
| **Final Propaganda deposit/standing** | R 6.1, 6.2.4, 6.3.5, 7.3. Cash can become an open Casino or resources after opening victory check. Final round omits Reset, so closed Casinos do not automatically reopen. | Strong lifecycle case; full round requires ordered decisions across four factions, earnings, Support and final ranking. A Cash-only fragment risks concealing the defining final-round task. Greater first-slice burden. |

For a conditional Propaganda arithmetic challenge, suppose Syndicate reaches its Cash-deposit decision with 7 open Casinos, 31 Resources and one Cash at a legal closed-Casino location. Taking 6 Resources gives margin `min(7−7, 37−30)=0`; opening the Casino gives `min(8−7, 31−30)=1`. The opening victory check has already passed; final ranking follows after Support, not immediately on the deposit. This is a local expectation, not a fully specified round fixture.

### Recommended task boundary

Start in a prepared midgame position after M26 has chosen a full Terror Operation and before resolving it. Permit legal Terror location/order choices, optional Kidnap at its permitted time, optional extension after its result, and deliberate Operation completion. Stop after this Operation and accompanying activity resolve; display the next decision/permission but do not claim to play that faction or finish the card. Earlier choices among Event, Pass and other Operations are outside this **already-selected-operation** boundary, not falsely absent legal actions.

Use no Cash, no persistent modifiers and no last-Event restriction. A City without a Casino allows only the Government target and avoids Casino-ransom ambiguity. Place M26 Underground Guerrillas above Police count and give Government sufficient Resources to guarantee the first Terror can be funded. A second eligible population-bearing location makes the proceeds matter to a subsequent decision. #4 must supply the entire map, resources, eligibility/current/preview context, supplies and validity reasoning, not just these local conditions.

Independent conditional expectation: at zero M26 Resources, Kidnap in neutral Santiago with two Underground M26 Guerrillas versus one Police and Government Resources 6. A die result 3 transfers 3, leaving Government 3; Terror costs 1, activates one M26 Guerrilla, places Terror and moves neutral population 1 to Passive Opposition. M26 retains 2 Resources and gains 1 Opposition contribution. With a second eligible location, the player can now spend again or finish. A result 1 funds only the first paid Terror absent a transfer. These are source-derived expectations, not chosen random outcomes for a deterministic game promise.

Do not omit R 1.4.1 voluntary own-piece removal or R 1.5.1 legal transfers to make this small. #4 should expose legal numeric transfers with giver/receiver ownership through local human decisions; external faction seats and proclamation presentation remain deferred. Removing the last necessary Underground Guerrilla or giving away needed funds cannot leave a committed Kidnap without its required Terror. The exact safe decision/commit boundary needs source-backed closure in #4; a synthetic prohibition must be labeled a narrowing, not official rules. A Cash-ransom variant is excluded from this starting position, not declared illegal generally. Cash is not spendable Resources, and a used Special Activity prevents Launder; do not claim it funds the City Terror.
