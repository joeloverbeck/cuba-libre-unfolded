This foundation records the owner's founding request and clarifications of 2026-11-14. The [constitution](../principles/principles-for-repos.md) controls; consulted families are `AUTH`, `PURP`, `WORK`, `EVID`, `SEM`, `REP`, `BOUND`, `INT`, `AGENT`, `OPS`, `PUB`, `GOV`, and `ADOPT`. Owner: `joeloverbeck`. Publication authority is explicit for this foundation set, MCP setup, and a public repository named `cuba-libre-unfolded`; publication does not establish gameplay or visual acceptance.

## Intended experience

Purpose class: recreation and personal utility. The intended benefit is playing Cuba Libre with its asymmetric faction objectives and negotiations, while the application performs rules bookkeeping and makes the situation and legal choices understandable. External Claude Code/Codex players can occupy any seats handed to them. Period-inspired faction proclamations make political communication part of the experience.

The owner's binding selections are Cuba Libre, Rust, reuse across the unfolded games, VectorCraft-authored visuals, three-dimensional pieces resembling the physical components, imported card faces, no built-in bots, and external agent seats. A successful product must support an intelligible game and worthwhile interaction with the other factions. A correct transition, attractive map, or convincing proclamation alone cannot demonstrate that experience.

The accepted initial complete product is the standard four-faction game, with the human able to choose any faction, external agents controlling the remaining seats, and save/resume. Platform and language are Ubuntu and English. Human takeover remains available; an absent agent must not force an automatic move or substitute bot.

## Product boundary and acceptance

One pipeline: pinned rules and selected assets plus human/agent decisions → Cuba Libre's deterministic rules authority → visible game consequences, proclamations, and recoverable match state → owner inspection and acceptance. Invalid input fails visibly without mutating the match. The owner maintains and consumes this product; its application, art, and packaging share its release and abandonment decision. Editor products and `unfolded-tabletop` retain their independent ownership.

Excluded: a general COIN engine, other COIN games, built-in decision bots or automated Non-Player rules, optional variants, hosted matchmaking, remote human multiplayer, automatic model spending, and a new art editor. Public visibility does not create a support or multiplatform commitment.

Acceptance of the later game requires independent rules evidence and a representative native game task with the owner, including decisions, communication, and save/resume. Subsequent expansion must obtain the constitution's second-use and return-use evidence where those claims apply. The first implementation issue must choose a source-derived difficult interaction, not declare a convenient tutorial sufficient for the full fidelity promise.
